## Summary
Created complete YOLOv8 training infrastructure for baseball bat detection,
replacing the HSV color-based approach with a learning-based model.

## What's New
- Complete YOLO training pipeline in `ai-service/yolo-bat-detection/`
- GPU-accelerated training (10 min vs 30-60 min on CPU)
- Trained model achieving 44.4% mAP@50 (baseline for iteration)
- Comprehensive scripts for training, validation, inference, and export

## Training Results
- **Dataset**: 749 images (603 train / 96 valid / 50 test)
- **Model**: YOLOv8n (3M parameters)
- **Training Time**: 10 minutes on GTX 1660 SUPER
- **Performance**:
  - mAP@50: 44.4%
  - Precision: 58.8%
  - Recall: 46.2%
  - Inference: 14.2ms per frame
- **Model Location**: `C:\dm\runs\detect\runs\detect\bat_detection2\weights\best.pt`
